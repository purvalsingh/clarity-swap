Why Clarity Swap

  1. Plain words. No "Ultra", "JIT" or "V1 transactions". One Auto mode that just works.
  2. Dollars first. Fees, value and impact in $, next to the price.
  3. Protection as a promise. "You'll get at least X, or nothing happens."
  4. Safety checks. Unverified tokens are stopped with live data: age, holders, liquidity.
  5. Failures explained. If a swap doesn't land, you see why and what to do next.
  6. Review before wallet. Tells you exactly what your wallet should show.

Quotes, swaps, balances and token data come from Jupiter's public API. Your keys never leave your wallet.
